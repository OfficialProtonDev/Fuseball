How it plays

  1. Got the bomb? Run into someone to tag them, or click to throw it at them.
  2. Missed throws fly back to you, and there are no instant tag-backs.
  3. It glows hotter as the fuse burns. The exact moment is always a guess.
  4. The blast catches anyone close by. Last one standing takes the round, and the court shrinks as people drop.
  5. Everyone except the winner drafts a perk. Perks stack. First to 3 rounds wins.
